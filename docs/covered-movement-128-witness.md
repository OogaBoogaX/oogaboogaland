# Camera sight voxel witnesses: issue #128

Rotating the camera invalidates rock-guide camera visibility and repeats terrain traversal to fixed surface samples. The fresh sampled profile identified terrain sight as the largest non-idle cost: its two largest baseline stacks had 586 and 446 self-samples; the later source/profile analysis located 898 terrain self-samples under `rock-guides.updateSurfaceBranch`.

This candidate stores one previously hit full voxel per camera surface sample. Reuse checks that the cell is still occupied and that the current segment crosses a shrunken interior for a positive interval above traversal tolerance. Otherwise the unchanged traversal runs. No clear result, convex window fragment or ramp hit is cached. Changing near-plane starts, camera origin/target and voxel occupancy cannot reuse a stale visibility boolean. Existing aperture and moving-object handling stays on its original path.

Storage is fixed at registration: one Int32 slot per authored sample (223,156 bytes in the tested registry), cleared on reset, released on disposal, and freshly initialized on re-entry. The frame path allocates nothing. Diagnostic counters expose cache queries/hits without altering acceptance checks.

Separate engine follow-up to investigation PR #165 and issue #128. Baseline: `9b7db4d7488f9909fc3d8f1b9e4d988dbb9090a4`; candidate: `d3743fc26afc4eea28cdb43b11158354671fa53b`. The unproven offset experiment is separate and absent from this branch.

Validation: build, touched-file syntax and whitespace checks pass. Final deterministic unit checks: 204/204; 14,555 comparisons against the uncached query with no mismatches, including near-plane suffixes, changed eyes/endpoints, reversed rays, authored windows/ramps, randomized voxel occupancy/mutations, zero-length rays and boundary tangencies. Randomized voxel cases recorded 313 witness hits. Storage disposal/re-entry checks pass. A setup error in initial testing (hole-guide allocation) was corrected before this final result.
