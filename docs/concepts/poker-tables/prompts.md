# Seated table art prompts

Built-in image generation supplied the tabletop artwork. The responsive HUD prototypes use the same captured practice flop, chip counts and character portraits with the game’s vector deck. Each prototype freezes after one betting decision so reviewers can replay it.

Use case: stylized-concept. One image tile for an original browser game's tabletop art review. Render ONLY an isolated horizontal oval poker tabletop, precisely centered, orthographic near-vertical top-down (like plan view), long axis horizontal; oval occupies 94% of width and 86% of height within a wide 3:2 landscape canvas. Entire oval and fixtures visible, flat uniform nearly-black studio background #101613. Art direction: warm, meticulously crafted Art Deco Bitcoin club, low-poly modeled miniatures with bevels, soft directional light from upper left, visibly tactile wool, leather and metal. Physical trim and little fixtures must read at thumbnail size. Absolutely empty matte felt in inner 72% of ellipse, uniformly lit. No writing, seats, humans, dealer, chips, playing cards, numbers, buttons, UI, badges or typography; the usable interface will be composited separately. No perspective front view or legs.

## Satoshi

Forest green founder's wool (#315442), a generous ring of 12 bookmatched mitred walnut marquetry leaves with pencil-thin brass borders, stitched oxblood red leather outer cushion (#833639). Little brass chip flutes and mint marks at the rail. Tiny block-zero escutcheon at twelve o'clock. Gold fan crest detail. Quietest and most finely crafted of the ten.

## Running Bitcoin

Charcoal olive wool (#343933), tobacco-brown rail (#574538). Two small recognizable early beige CRT terminals with chunky keyboards, amber phosphor screen marks, perched symmetrically on the upper left and upper right curves, viewed from above at a slight angle. Three parallel copper serial traces on the verge and seventeen punched amber paper-tape dots along the near lip. The effect of an early home computer translated into club furniture.

## Lightning

Deep Prussian blue wool (#173b5d), midnight navy rail (#29344b). A pair of pale electric-blue faceted quartz endpoints at the upper left and upper right rail, a short cyan conductor between, twelve forked silver circuit traces with tiny cyan solder pads around the narrow selvedge. Crisp isolated highlights on a dark blue oval, quiet matte center, no lightning crossing the playing field.

## Proof of Work

Umber wool (#484037), dark espresso leather (#42342d). On the upper-left rim a compact physical mechanical counter, two hex odometer drums, an exposed six-spoke copper wheel and comb-like vertical cooling fins. Alternating copper and amber diagonal index ticks at the felt border. Industrial tabletop instrument with chunky silhouette, clean empty field.

## Pizza Day

Olive wool (#394b33), oxblood leather. Narrow inlaid alternating tomato-red and toasted ivory enamel diamonds ring the inner lip. At the upper-left rail sits a little eight-slice pepperoni pizza on a low round brass baker's peel with handle. Charming miniature, not an enormous pizza. Keep the felt plain, subtle and premium.

## The Halving

Burgundy wine felt (#5e3635), russet leather rail (#5b3532). A standing medal at upper-left rim depicts a sun sliced precisely in two, one bronze semicircle and one gold semicircle with a sliver of dark between, in a thin halo and upper rays. Four nested arcs of warm brass and copper run around the selvedge, each half the length of the previous one and ending in a tiny stud. The center is undisturbed oxblood wine.

## Fee Market

Petrol teal wool (#23484b) and brown bronze leather (#574537). Along the back-left rail is a miniature exchange skyline: nine slender amber glass skyscrapers of varying heights on one narrow black plinth, flanked by small dark bronze masonry exchange houses. Low copper city-block outlines and amber window inlays ring the edge. Keep miniature architecture outside playing area.

## Twenty One Million

Blue charcoal felt (#292d39), near-black plum lacquered leather rail (#201e28). A small black-and-gold assay balance with two hanging gold pans mounted on the upper-left rim in silhouette. On a single precise thin gold orbit round the felt are twenty small ivory rectangular assay ingots plus one larger terminal amber ingot. Sparse restrained jeweler's craftsmanship. Do not put coins in the center.

## Taproot

Deep verdigris green wool (#1f5043), moss-green leather rail (#3f4b38). One small sculptural copper wire binary tree on the upper-left rim with bifurcating angular branches and clusters of faceted malachite and jade leaves. Fine copper rootlets branch along the selvedge, terminating in mint dots. Crisp botanical geometry, no foliage on the card field.

## Mt. Gox

Storm blue slate felt (#324049), smoke plum-black rail (#353038). A broad band of individually broken dark slate slabs just inside the cushion. Distinct stepped fractures are held together by small two-footed brass repair staples, visible from above. A single short descending brass stair-step motif on the near right. Hints of high-roller rosso marble at the outermost lip. Elegant repaired stone, intact clear wool center, no ruin or rubble.
