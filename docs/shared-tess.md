# Shared Tess on the island

Signed-in contributors in the live island room share one Tess. Her greeting offers **Join queue**; it does not reserve a turn. Tapping her opens the same panel. A waiting visitor sees their position and can explore the island or leave the queue. At the front, **Talk to Tess** must be accepted within 20 seconds. Only then does Tess approach and offer questions or a tour. A granted turn offers unread island news first, using the same per-login history as the local guide; Show me or Not now records it as seen. Joining, waiting, accepting a turn or leaving an unanswered offer does not mark news as read. With no unread news she offers the island tour. Conversation text stays in the existing private browser/HTTP path; the room never receives questions, answers or camera positions. Other visitors follow Tess's visible position and beam with their own cameras unchanged.

The Room advertises `tess-guide-v1`. `worker/src/tess-queue.js` owns a bounded FIFO of 32 accounts, one entry per account and one ready/active turn. `join` is idempotent. `accept`, `cancel`, `touch` and `pose` require the requesting account's current token; no identity is accepted from a payload. Ready prompts expire after 20 seconds, idle conversations after 60 seconds, and active turns after five minutes even with activity. Waiting visitors keep their FIFO place while connected, visible and on the island, however long earlier turns take; no waiting heartbeat or periodic storage write is added. The ready prompt still requires acceptance within 20 seconds, so an unattended visitor cannot hold the front. A dropped connection, hidden tab or departure holds the place for 15 seconds; explicit cancellation and revoked access release it immediately. Reconnection within that grace keeps the account's place. Tours report activity while running, and typing or asking a question also renews the idle deadline. Cancellation and lease expiry abort pending answers and return the visitor's controls.

Only structural queue changes and occasional activity extensions write `tessQueue` in Durable Object storage, in mutation order. Hibernation retains leases; a deployment with no surviving sockets starts the same short reconnect grace. The existing alarm wakes at the nearest lease deadline; an empty queue adds no wake or timer. Queue controls are limited to four per second per connection. The active account's bounded 12-number visual pose is relayed at most four times per second, never saved as conversation history. Other accounts cannot publish it. Each visitor receives only their own token and position, plus the public busy/count/pose state.

Signed-out visitors, standalone pages and older rooms retain the existing local guide. Other scenes' Tess guides are unchanged. A previously shared guide pauses on a lost connection instead of silently starting a separate tour.

Validation: Worker queue checks cover duplicates, FIFO, unauthorized tokens/poses, bounds, cancellation, expiry, reconnect, hibernation and deployment recovery. The existing hub guide browser session includes explicit joining, readiness, private local answers, spectator camera independence and disconnect cleanup. Live deployment acceptance requires separate signed-in contributor browsers.
